PageSift privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Applies to the PageSift Chrome extension.

Short version: PageSift has no server. It doesn't collect, sell or share your data. When you click Extract or Summarize, the text of the current page is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter), using your own API key.

What PageSift handles

DataWhere it goesHow long
Your API key, chosen provider, model and settings Saved in your browser with chrome.storage.local. Not synced to your Google account. The key is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Until you change it, click Delete all PageSift data, or remove the extension.
Text and links of the page you're on (or just your selection) Read only when you click a button, and sent once to your chosen AI provider to produce the table or summary. PageSift keeps nothing after it shows the result. The provider's own retention policy applies (see below).
The last result for each tab chrome.storage.session, in memory, so the result is still there if the popup closes. Cleared when the tab closes or the browser quits.
Your last column list and instructions chrome.storage.local in your browser. Until changed or deleted.

What PageSift does not do

  • No PageSift server, account, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking pixels.
  • It doesn't read pages in the background or keep a browsing history. It can only see a tab after you click the toolbar icon (Chrome's activeTab permission).
  • It doesn't sell or transfer data to third parties, or use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to producing the result you asked for.
  • It doesn't load remote code. All code ships inside the extension.

Your AI provider

Page content you send is processed by the provider you selected, under your own account and its terms: OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter. Don't run PageSift on pages with information you aren't allowed to share with that provider.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: read the current page's text, only after you click.
  • storage: save your settings and key in your browser.
  • Optional access to api.openai.com, api.anthropic.com or openrouter.ai: requested only for the provider you pick, so PageSift can send it requests.

Deleting your data

Open PageSift settings and click Delete all PageSift data, or remove the extension. Both erase everything PageSift stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Questions: use the support link on PageSift's Chrome Web Store page.
